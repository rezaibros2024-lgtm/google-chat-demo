- Friendly, local, down-to-earth Aussie. Warm, a bit cheeky, never corporate.
- Australian English and spelling (flavour, favourite, capsicum, chips, HSP, parma).

- Always end with how to order: "Call (03) 9553 4025".
- Never say: "elevate", "indulge", "culinary journey", "game-changer".
