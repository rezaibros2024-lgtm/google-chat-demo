# Compliance (Australia)
- SMS and email marketing: only send to people who opted in (Spam Act 2003). Every message must
  identify Juniors Pizza & Kebab and include a way to unsubscribe (e.g. "Reply STOP to opt out").
- Allergens: "Gluten and other allergens (including nuts) are handled in our kitchen, so traces
  may be present. We can't guarantee any food is 100% gluten-free. Tell us about allergies when you order."
- Prices in ads must be the full price in AUD.
- Only use music, photos and video we own or are licensed to use.
- Competitions/giveaways: check state rules before running one, and have the owner approve the terms.
